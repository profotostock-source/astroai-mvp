# Inner Compass AI

## Product Vision

Inner Compass AI is a premium AI-powered platform that creates deeply personalized life reports using structured self-reflection, AI interpretation, and astrology as one of its analytical engines.

The goal is not to predict the future.

The goal is to help people better understand themselves, discover strengths, identify growth opportunities, and make thoughtful decisions.

---

# Target Audience

People interested in:

- Self-development
- Psychology
- Personal growth
- Coaching
- Spirituality
- Astrology

Age:

25–55

---

# MVP

Telegram Bot

Flow:

1. User starts the bot
2. Enters:
   - Name
   - Birth date
   - Birth time
   - Birth place
3. System calculates natal chart
4. AI generates interpretation
5. PDF report is created
6. PDF is sent back to Telegram

# AI Rules

The AI must never:

- Predict unavoidable events
- Say that destiny is fixed
- Use fear to influence decisions

Instead it should:

- Explain tendencies
- Offer practical recommendations
- Encourage reflection
- Maintain a calm, supportive and respectful tone
